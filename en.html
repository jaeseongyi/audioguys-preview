Before Getting Started

Eligibility for Distribution & Use

Can anyone apply for music distribution?

Anyone from solo artists to teams and labels can apply. Once you share your music with us, we'll listen together and explore the best path forward for distribution and collaboration. We are here to consult on new digital releases, physical album distribution, and even transferring existing catalogs from other distributors.

What genres of music do you distribute?

We don't limit ourselves to any genre. Audioguy transforms all music into spatial audio and shares it with the world.

Which platforms will it be distributed to?

We distribute to dozens of platforms, including major global services and key local platforms both in Korea and worldwide. This includes Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, TIDAL, Amazon Music, Deezer, as well as Melon, Bugs, VIBE, Genie, FLO, Kakao Music, and many more.

Can I apply with my unreleased debut track as well?

Yes! Artists preparing for their first release are more than welcome to apply. Once you send us the music you're working on, we'll listen to it together and explore release and collaboration opportunities with you.

Can I also get a consultation regarding physical album distribution?

Yes! We can consult on both digital music distribution and physical album distribution. If you share your album details and current progress with us, we'll gladly review the next steps together.

Can I also transfer music that was released through another distributor?

Yes! Tracks that are eligible for transfer after their previous distribution agreement has expired can be moved to Audioguy. We will verify your existing release details and confirm the contract termination before proceeding with the transfer. Plus, we also offer full support for spatial audio production.

Will my existing play counts be kept if I switch distributors?

We will verify your existing identification codes, such as the track's ISRC and the album's UPC, along with your audio files and release information, to help transfer and link your previous play history. Please note, however, that the final linking may vary depending on each streaming platform's matching criteria and processing results.

Can I also apply for content rights management on YouTube and Meta?

Yes! For most releases, we support registration for YouTube Content ID and Meta's Rights Manager. This helps you manage your rights and monetize your music whenever it's used across these platforms. You can choose whether to apply before your release, though please note that registration may be restricted or rejected depending on the nature of your music, your scope of rights ownership, and each platform's specific policies.

Release & Document Submission

What materials do I need to prepare to request a release?

Please prepare our release form along with your album description, audio files, album artwork, artist bio, and profile materials. If it feels like too much to fill out right away, feel free to just send over your tracklist, audio files, and album artwork first. A dedicated manager will be happy to help you complete the release details.

By when should I submit my request relative to the desired release date?

We recommend allowing about four weeks from the completion of spatial audio production to your release date. If you reach out to us about a month and a half before your desired release date, we'll gladly guide you through the production and release preparation process.

What format should I use to submit the audio files?

Please submit your files in WAV, AIFF, or FLAC format. We recommend an audio quality of 24-bit/44.1kHz or higher.

What are the required specifications for submitting the album cover?

Please prepare an image that is at least 3,000 × 3,000px and 150ppi. We recommend 4,500 × 4,500px at 300ppi, and please submit your file in JPG or PNG format.

Can I still apply if I don't have an ISRC or UPC?

Yes! Audioguy will issue the ISRC and UPC codes needed for your new release. If you're transferring previously released music, please provide us with your existing identification codes.

Can I change the release details or release date after submitting my request?

Whether changes can be made depends on the current delivery status on the platform. If you need to make any updates, please reach out to your dedicated manager. We’ll check the status and guide you through the available options.

Can I edit the title, credits, or cover art after release?

Yes, you can request changes even after release. However, since the availability and timing of updates may vary depending on each platform's policies, we recommend submitting accurate information and materials during the initial registration.

How can I remove or stop the distribution of my released music?

Please request service termination by emailing us at ip@audioguyrecords.com. Once received, we will guide you through the next steps. If you wish to switch to another distributor after discontinuing the service, you may do so after your contract period has ended.

Spatial Audio Production

Is there an additional cost for spatial audio production?

No, it's not. We provide spatial audio production completely free of charge for music distributed through Audioguy.

Can already released music also be produced in spatial audio?

Yes, absolutely! You can also consult with us about spatial audio production for your previously released music. We also support spatial audio production for tracks being transferred to Audioguy from other distributors.

What files are needed for spatial audio production?

Please send us your stem files and final stereo mix. Our engineers will use the stereo mix as a reference to create your spatial audio.

Can spatial audio be produced using only a finished stereo track?

Yes! Even without stem files, we can create spatial audio directly from your final stereo mix using our proprietary AI-SPHR technology.

Can I request revisions to the produced spatial audio?

Please note that revisions are not included with our complimentary spatial audio production. If you would like to request revisions, they are available for an additional fee, and we will be happy to provide pricing per track upon request.

Will the spatial audio and stereo versions be released together?

Yes. Audioguy releases Dolby Atmos tracks alongside their stereo versions. We deliver both versions together, and each platform serves the format it supports.

Where can I listen to the spatial audio I created?

You can enjoy it on platforms that support spatial audio, including Apple Music, TIDAL, Amazon Music, Melon, Genie, and VIBE.

Can I also request recording, mixing, and mastering together?

Yes! Recording, mixing, and mastering services can also be requested separately. The process and costs may vary depending on the track and the scope of work, so please feel free to reach out to us at ip@audioguyrecords.com for a consultation.

Settlement & Expenses

Are there any initial costs to get started with distribution?

No, you can start distribution with no upfront costs, and spatial audio production is also provided free of charge.

How are distribution profits shared?

We pay 70% of music revenue settled through domestic and international platforms and distribution partners directly to rights holders. The same rate applies to any revenue generated from spatial audio versions produced by Audioguy.

When and how often are settlements processed?

We'll send you a settlement statement every quarter. Once your accumulated balance reaches 100,000 KRW or more, you can request a payout whenever you'd like.

When can I check my first settlement?

Domestic platform revenue can be viewed in the next settlement quarter following your release. Due to varying settlement schedules of international platforms and distribution partners, international revenue may take longer to reflect than domestic revenue. Please rest assured that this is simply a difference in settlement timelines rather than missing revenue, and all settlement details will be updated sequentially as they are received.

Will my settlement be paid automatically?

Settlement payouts are processed upon request. Once you review your statement and your balance reaches 100,000 KRW or more, please feel free to request a payout whenever you're ready.

What documents do I need to submit to receive my payout?

When signing the contract, please submit a copy of your ID and bankbook, or a copy of your business registration certificate and bankbook.

Can the payment be split among multiple participants?

As a rule, settlement payouts are made to the primary artist or rights holder who signed the contract.

Where can I check my settlement details?

You can check this on Audioguys for Artists. Once you log in with your rights holder account, you can easily view your monthly settlement trends, as well as track your performance by period and platform.

Contracts & Rights

Does signing a distribution contract transfer the rights to my music?

You will retain full master rights to your original sound recordings. However, the master rights to the spatial audio versions produced by Audioguy belong to the company. Revenue generated from the spatial audio versions will be paid out to rightsholders using the same royalty distribution rate.

Can I sign a contract on an album-by-album basis?

Yes, Audioguy's music distribution contracts are signed on an album-by-album basis.

Can I also distribute cover songs?

Yes, you can distribute cover songs as long as you have obtained the necessary rights and approvals. When submitting your application, please provide the required documentation to verify these rights, such as copyright clearance letters or arrangement consent forms. You can download the relevant forms from the KOMCA (Korea Music Copyright Association) resource center.

Can I distribute music that uses purchased beats or samples?

Yes, please submit the relevant documentation—such as a copyright consent agreement, clearance form, or derivative work authorization—verifying your right to use any beats or samples. You can find the relevant forms in the KOMCA (Korea Music Copyright Association) archive.

Can I also submit music created with AI?

First-time releasing artists are more than welcome to consult with us. Based on your music, rights information, and prepared materials, we’ll guide you through the feasibility and necessary steps.

Can I also submit music created with AI?

Yes. You can distribute your release as long as you disclose the use of AI during the application process and submit the required declaration and supporting documents. Please note that an AI indicator may be displayed depending on each streaming platform's policy.

First-time releasing artists are more than welcome to consult with us. Based on your music, rights information, and prepared materials, we’ll guide you through the feasibility and necessary steps.

Inquiries & User Guide

How can I get in touch for a consultation about distribution or production?

Feel free to send your music along with any questions to ip@audioguyrecords.com. Whether it's about new releases, catalog transfers, physical distribution, spatial audio, or recording, mixing, and mastering services, we'll review your needs and guide you through the process.

Who holds the rights to the original audio and the spatial audio version?

The master rights to the original stereo tracks remain with the original rights holders. The master rights (sound recording copyright) for the Dolby Atmos versions produced by Audioguy will be retained by Audioguy.

Can I switch to another distributor once my contract ends?

Once the contract ends, you can transfer the original stereo audio files to another distributor. Audioguy will provide the release codes, metadata, and registration history needed for the transfer. Please note that the Dolby Atmos version produced by Audioguy is not eligible for transfer.
